What we are

Classified

The Midnight Waffle Society is a monthly conspiracy of night people, carb loyalists, and the incurably curious.

Once a month we commandeer a place that has no business hosting a waffle service — a laundromat, a rooftop greenhouse, the mezzanine of a parking garage — and we run a griddle line until the batter runs out. There is no menu. There is the waffle of the month, and there is a topping table operated on the honor system.

We are not a restaurant. We are not a brand activation. We are forty strangers standing in warm yellow light at one in the morning, holding something crisp, agreeing not to explain it to anyone who wasn't there.

Attendance is capped by griddle throughput. The griddle does not negotiate.
